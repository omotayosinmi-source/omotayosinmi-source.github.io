Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Digital Autonomous ("we", "us", "our") — full registered company details to be inserted here once confirmed. For any question about this policy or about your personal data, contact hello@digitalautonomous.co.uk.

For the purposes of UK data protection law we act as a controller for data you send us directly, and as a processor for personal data we handle inside a client's systems on that client's instructions. Where we act as a processor, our client's own privacy notice governs that data and a written data processing agreement sets out the terms.

What this website collects

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and includes no third-party analytics, advertising or social tracking scripts.

  • Contact form. The form on our contact page does not transmit anything to us. It assembles a message and opens your own email application so that you can review and send it. Nothing is stored by the website.
  • Email you send us. If you email us, we receive your address, your message and anything you attach.
  • Audit request form. The audit form collects a name, telephone number and email address so that we can contact you to arrange the audit you asked for, and for nothing else.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry — legitimate interests, and steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract.
  • To provide services to clients — performance of a contract.
  • To arrange the audit you asked for — consent, which you give by submitting the form and can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep the site secure and available — legitimate interests.

How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted. Audit request details are kept only for as long as needed to arrange and follow up that audit. A specific retention schedule will be published here once confirmed.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.
