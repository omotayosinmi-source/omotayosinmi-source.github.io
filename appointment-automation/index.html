What it fixes

Appointment Automation

Booking by phone tag wastes a remarkable amount of time, and appointments nobody reminded turn into empty slots that cannot be resold.

What we build

What is actually included.

Every build is scoped to your business, but this is the shape of it.

Real availability, not a guess

Reads your live calendar, honours buffers, working hours, resource and staff rules.

Booked in the conversation

The slot is offered and taken during the call or the text thread, while intent is highest.

Confirmations immediately

Sent by the channel the customer used, with everything they need to turn up in the right place.

Reminders on a schedule

Timed to your service — the day before, the morning of, whatever reduces no-shows for you.

Self-service reschedule and cancel

One link. Cancellations free the slot automatically so it can be filled again.

Business outcome

What changes for you.

Written as outcomes rather than percentages, because we will not publish a number we cannot evidence. When we have measured results from clients, they will appear on the case studies page with the method stated.

  • Less phone tag to arrange a single appointment
  • Fewer forgotten appointments
  • Cancelled slots released instead of sitting empty
  • Bookings taken outside office hours

How it works

Audit, design, build, deploy, optimise.

A clear path with a decision point before anything gets built. You see what is worth automating — and what is not — before you commit.

STEP 01

Audit

We map where enquiries are lost and where time goes, then show you what is worth automating first and what is not.

STEP 02

Design

We agree exactly what the system will do, what it will never do, and where it hands over to a person.

STEP 03

Build

We build it around the tools you already pay for. No migration, no new platform for your team to learn.

STEP 04

Deploy

We test against your real workflows and edge cases before a single customer touches it, then launch.

STEP 05

Optimise

We monitor it in production, fix what breaks and refine what works, as an ongoing service.
